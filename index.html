About

Multipolar Front is a personal automation project for managing YouTube Shorts publishing on its owner's own YouTube channel. It is operated solely by its owner and is not offered as a service to other users.

Privacy Policy

Multipolar Front is used only by its owner. It processes content and comments from public Telegram channels the owner belongs to; it does not access private Telegram conversations. It uses the official YouTube API, authorized through the owner's own channel credential, to upload videos, read comments on that channel, and post replies. These YouTube functions use authorized API access, not scraping. Public source content, comment data, and processing records may be stored locally to support the automation. The app does not request access to other users' accounts or sell their data. Videos and replies selected for publication are sent to YouTube. Public comments may contain personal information even though they are publicly visible.
